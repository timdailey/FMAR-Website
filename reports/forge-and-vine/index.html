What Schema Is Forge and Vine Missing?
Schema markup (invisible code on your website that helps AI systems read facts about your business) is added to your website by your web developer or a WordPress plugin like Yoast or Rank Math. You cannot see it when you visit a website, but AI can. Think of it as a translation layer between your website and AI platforms — it tells them exactly what type of business you are, where you are located, what your hours are, and what your customers think of you. Without it, AI platforms have to guess at these facts from your regular webpage text, and they often cannot find what they need.
