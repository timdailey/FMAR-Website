The category

What an AI Visibility Platform Should Actually Do

"AI visibility platform," "AEO tool," and "GEO platform" all describe the same emerging category: software that tracks how often, how accurately, and how favorably AI systems mention a brand, then tells you what to do about it. If you're evaluating this category for the first time, four things separate a platform worth paying for from one that just looks impressive in a demo.

  • Multi-engine coverage. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews each weight credibility differently. A platform tracking only one gives you an incomplete picture.
  • Citation-level attribution, not just brand-mention tracking. Knowing your name appeared somewhere is not the same as knowing which page or source got pulled.
  • Entity signal visibility across the third-party sources — Wikipedia, G2, Crunchbase, Google Business Profile — that AI systems check to verify a brand is real.
  • A path to action, not just a dashboard. Data without a prioritized fix list is a reporting tool, not a visibility platform.
