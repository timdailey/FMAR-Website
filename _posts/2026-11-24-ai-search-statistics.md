This page collects AI search statistics we could verify at the original source. Each number is one sentence with a link to the study or announcement it comes from, so you can check it and cite it. The figures were last verified on October 31, 2026, and we revisit the page as new studies are published.

A few ground rules. We cite the original publisher rather than articles that repeat the number. We include the sample, the date, and the data period wherever the source gives them. Several of these studies come from companies that sell SEO or AI visibility tools, and we say so. Where two studies disagree, we show both rather than choosing one.

## AI search usage

- Google CEO Sundar Pichai said on the company's July 22, 2026 earnings call that AI Mode has [surpassed 1 billion monthly active users](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/) since its global expansion in October 2025.
- In the same remarks, Pichai said the Gemini app has 950 million monthly active users, and that its daily users have roughly tripled over the past year ([Google](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/)).
- Pichai also described AI Mode, like AI Overviews, as producing an incremental increase in total Search queries ([Google](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/)).

## Clicks and traffic

- Pew Research Center found that users clicked a traditional result link in 8% of visits when a Google AI summary appeared, compared with 15% of visits without one ([Pew Research Center, July 22, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)).
- In the same Pew analysis, users clicked a link inside the AI summary itself in 1% of visits ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)).
- Pew also found that browsing sessions ended after the results page in 26% of visits with an AI summary versus 16% without ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)). The data covers 900 U.S. adults and 68,879 Google searches in March 2025.
- Ahrefs estimated that AI Overviews are associated with a 58% lower average click-through rate for the top-ranking page, comparing December 2023 with December 2025 across 300,000 keywords ([Ahrefs, February 4, 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/)).
- In the same study, the estimated click-through impact was 50.8% lower at position two and 19.4% lower at position ten ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/)).
- Ahrefs found the position-one click-through rate for keywords with an AI Overview fell from 0.073 to 0.016 between December 2023 and December 2025 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/)). The 58% figure compares actual click-through with a forecast of what it would have been, not a directly observed counterfactual.

## AI referral traffic

- SE Ranking, analyzing 101,574 websites with Google Analytics, found that AI referrals made up 0.32% of total website traffic in January to April 2026, up from 0.24% in 2025 and 0.02% in 2024 ([SE Ranking, June 18, 2026](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-study/)).
- In the same dataset, organic search made up 42.75% of total traffic, about 134 times AI referral traffic, down from roughly 300 times at the start of 2025 ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-study/)).
- SE Ranking found ChatGPT accounted for 74.78% of AI referrals in 2026, followed by Gemini at 11.56%, Perplexity at 7.23%, Copilot at 3.51%, and Claude at 2.62% ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-study/)).
- Conductor reported that AI referral traffic was 1.08% of all website traffic across the 10 industries it analyzed, with ChatGPT supplying 87.4% of AI referral traffic ([Conductor, 2026 AEO / GEO Benchmarks Report](https://conductor.com/academy/aeo-geo-benchmarks-report/)). The AI search data covers May to September 2025.
- Conductor reports that information technology had the highest AI referral share among the industries it analyzed at 2.80% of traffic, followed by consumer staples at 1.91% ([Conductor](https://conductor.com/academy/aeo-geo-benchmarks-report/)).

The two studies differ because of their samples, time periods, and how AI visits are attributed. Treat the range of roughly 0.3% to 1.1% as a sign of how uncertain measurement is, not as a precise number for your site.

## Citations and sources

- Ahrefs found that, on average, only about 12% of links cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot appear in Google's top 10 results for the same prompt, across 15,000 long-tail prompts ([Ahrefs, August 11, 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/)).
- The same study found Perplexity's overlap with Google's top 10 was higher, at 28.6% ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/)). Ahrefs notes the ChatGPT data was collected in early July 2025 and may have changed.
- Writesonic, analyzing more than 1 million AI Overviews, estimated a 33.07% chance that the page ranking first in Google is cited in an AI Overview, falling to 13.04% for position ten ([Writesonic, 2025](https://writesonic.com/blog/ai-citations-from-serp-results-study)). The write-up does not fully describe its method, so treat the figure as directional.
- The Tow Center for Digital Journalism at Columbia tested eight AI search tools with 1,600 queries about news articles and found they collectively gave incorrect answers to more than 60% of queries ([Columbia Journalism Review, March 6, 2025](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php)).
- In the same test, Perplexity answered 37% of queries incorrectly, the lowest error rate of the eight tools ([Columbia Journalism Review](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php)). The test used short news excerpts, so it does not describe every kind of question.

## Brand visibility factors

- In a study of 75,000 brands, Ahrefs found that branded web mentions had the strongest correlation with brand visibility in AI Overviews, at 0.664 ([Ahrefs, May 26, 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)).
- In the same study, branded anchors correlated at 0.527, branded search volume at 0.392, Domain Rating at 0.326, and number of backlinks at 0.218 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)).
- Ahrefs states that correlation does not equal causation, and that many signals combine to influence AI search visibility ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)).

## Technical signals

- SE Ranking found that 10.13% of nearly 300,000 domains had an llms.txt file, and it found no correlation between having one and how often a domain is cited by AI models ([SE Ranking, October 15, 2026](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/)). For the full picture, see [llms.txt: what it is and whether it works]({{ '/blog/what-is-llms-txt/' | relative_url }}).
- Google states that no special optimization, new machine-readable files, or AI-specific schema.org markup is needed to appear in AI Overviews and AI Mode; a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)).

## How to use these statistics

- **Cite the original.** Link to the study, not to this page, and give the year and sample.
- **Match the claim to the evidence.** Most figures here are correlations or single-study estimates. Words like "associated with" and "found" are deliberate.
- **Watch the date.** AI search changes quickly, and a 2025 study may not describe today.
- **Pair the numbers with your own data.** Your citation rate and referral traffic matter more to your decisions than an industry average. ## Method and limits

We chose studies that publish a sample size and date, and we confirmed each figure on the original page. We left out figures we could only find on pages that repeat other people's numbers. We have not independently reproduced any study. Where a source's own text is inconsistent, we left the number out; for example, we did not use the share of AI Overview citations that come from Google's top 10, because the source states two slightly different values.
